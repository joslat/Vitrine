# Authors and credits

## Author

**José Luis Latorre Millas** — the idea, the creative direction, and the concept behind VITRINE
are his.

VITRINE is not a tutorial sample or a framework demonstration that happened to acquire an opinion.
The premise — that an agent demo is worth little unless it can *show its evidence*, and that the
interesting engineering lives in the guardrails, the abstention gates, the channel audit, and the
separation of a measured claim from an asserted one — is the author's, and it is what the rest of
the repository exists to serve. Specifically:

- **Idea and concept** — the evidence-first framing: a recommendation agent judged by what it can
  prove rather than by what it says, with every number traceable to the run that produced it.
- **Creative direction** — the personas, the catalogue, the scenarios, and the demo narrative;
  the control-room presentation; the voice of the documentation.
- **Design and architecture** — the guardrail pipeline, the one sanctioned answer channel, the
  offline/live arm separation, and the provider composition boundary.
- **Evaluation strategy** — the admitted-check contract, the mutation controls, the missingness
  semantics, and the refusal to present an estimate as a measurement.
- **Implementation, documentation, and publication curation.**

AI coding assistants supported implementation and review. Responsibility, authorship, and the
credit for the idea remain with the named human author.
